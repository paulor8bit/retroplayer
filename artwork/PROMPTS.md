# Recursos visuais do RetroPlayer 1.1

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada image_gen. Os PNGs finais estão em `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`.

## Capa — deck_cover.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square album artwork for native Android music app RetroPlayer. Create an exquisite dark retro-futurist 3D illustration: a translucent smoked-glass cassette tape floating at a slight angle above concentric vinyl-like grooves, luminous acid-lime tape reels, small warm orange accents, brushed charcoal metal details. Premium tactile product illustration, dramatic studio lighting, nearly black indigo background, crisp silhouette, centered subject with ample margin. Palette #0e1017, #c9ff66, #ff9966. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, square 1024px composition.

## Animação — cassette_sprites.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: animation sprite sheet for a native Android music player. Exactly 4 frames in a perfectly even 2 by 2 grid, each cell occupies an identical square with ample transparent margin. Same adorable miniature charcoal cassette-tape robot with lime-green tape reels as eyes, tiny headphones and small orange feet, tasteful polished 3D toy illustration. A subtle dance loop: top left neutral, top right leaning slightly right, bottom left neutral with arms raised, bottom right leaning slightly left. Every frame same scale, consistent face and cassette shell, feet aligned within respective cells, all parts fully contained in their cell. Transparent background across entire sheet, no cell borders, no labels, no shadows crossing cells, no text, no watermark. Square 1024x1024 sheet.

A animação usa a grade 2 × 2 diretamente no Canvas nativo, sem recortar ou alterar o PNG. Os ícones da interface e do aplicativo são vetores nativos para manter nitidez e acessibilidade.
